## Production Bible

STILL LEARNING is a ten-part documentary podcast (nine episodes plus a bonus) that teaches the full PhD thesis *Selective Reinitialization Algorithms for Preventing Plasticity Loss in Artificial Neural Networks* (Juan Fernando Hernandez-Garcia, University of Alberta, 2026, CC BY 4.0) through the guided book *Keeping Neural Networks Learning*. It pairs a FRONTLINE-style investigative spine with Planet Money-style explainers, toy experiments and personified characters. Every section, figure, table, equation and algorithm of the thesis is covered somewhere in the series.

### The premise in one line

The machines we build learn once and then freeze; when we make them keep learning, they slowly lose the ability to learn at all. This series investigates that failure, who saw it first, why it happens, and the cure: periodically giving small parts of the network a fresh start.

### Format

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Episodes | 9 core episodes + 1 bonus ("The Fine Print") |
| Running time | 40–55 minutes per episode (about 6,000–8,500 spoken words) |
| Structure | Cold open → title sting → Act One / Two / Three (+ Four where needed) → "Three Things to Remember" → "Check Your Understanding" quiz → credits + next-episode tease |
| Tone | FRONTLINE gravitas in narration; Planet Money warmth, jokes and props in explainers; never condescending |
| Companion page | Every episode ends with SHOW NOTES: each equation written out, each figure summarized, thesis section references |

### Ground rules for writers

1. **Accuracy first.** Every number, setting and finding comes from the book. Approximate figure readings are flagged as "read off the chart."
2. **Real people are named, never voiced.** The thesis author, his supervisor and every cited researcher appear only by name and citation. No invented quotes.
3. **Equations are spoken, then printed.** A host reads each equation in words, Lucía or Tomás explains each symbol, and the SHOW NOTES print it in full.
4. **Figures become "audio figures."** Dev or Lucía describes axes, colors and the shape of each curve, then states what it shows.
5. **Analogies are labelled and stress-tested.** Capt. Al-Harbi or a host says where each analogy breaks.
6. **Slips are flagged.** THE AUDITOR handles every correction the book notes.
7. **Attribution.** Each episode's credits restate that the thesis is © Juan Fernando Hernandez Garcia, 2026, CC BY 4.0, adapted here; code at github.com/JFernando4/plasticity-via-reinit.
